Review autopilot uses the existing review-reply dispatch path and its persisted idempotency key. A reply must be ready, positive, within the configured rating threshold, at most 800 characters, and contain no links. The four-star skip setting is preserved. An explicit manual or forbidden reply-tool override blocks automatic publication on every platform, including Telegram and VK.

Yandex Maps additionally requires a known publication date within the past seven days and a Redis-backed limit of 10 automatic attempts per business per one-hour window. A missing or unavailable Redis counter leaves the reply for manual handling. The window begins with its first attempt; failed RPA attempts also consume capacity.

Supported Yandex dates are RFC3339 timestamps, ISO dates (`2026-11-03`), numeric dates (`03.11.2026`), and Russian calendar labels: `сегодня`, `вчера`, `3 ноября`, and `3 ноября 2025`. Calendar labels use Moscow time. A date without a year refers to the most recent matching date, including the previous year across New Year. Unknown or invalid dates never establish automatic eligibility; ingestion time is not a substitute. Background sync and chat-tool ingestion retain the parsed publication date in platform metadata.

Only a freshly generated eligible reply that reaches the hourly cap receives the deferred marker. Existing ready drafts do not start sending when the feature is enabled. A later sync pass retries marked replies using their saved draft and rechecks all gates. If a marked review becomes ineligible, its marker is cleared so it cannot block the next page. Manual decisions, failed sends and successful sends remain outside automatic retry selection. The automatic receipt is recorded only after successful publication.
